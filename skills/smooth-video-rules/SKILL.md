# Smooth Video Rules

Seamless flow is what makes a video feel professional. Follow these rules on every video unless the owner asks otherwise.

## 1. Transitions
- Never put black frames, fades to black or dips to black between shots. They read as choppy.
- Default to a straight cut on motion or a short blend (about 0.2 to 0.5 seconds). Match direction, color and movement across the cut when you can.
- Use a hard smash cut only when the cut itself is the joke or the beat. Say so in your notes when you do.
- Keep color and brightness consistent shot to shot so nothing jumps.

## 2. On-screen text
- Hold any text at least 2 seconds, longer for more than about 6 words (a good rule is 2 seconds plus about 0.3 seconds per extra word).
- Opening titles hold about 2 seconds before anything else changes, so they can actually be read.
- Keep text inside safe margins (about 5 to 8% from each edge), high contrast, and never covering faces or the key action.
- One idea per card. Don't stack captions on top of titles.

## 3. Narration and pacing
- Read at a natural, easy-to-follow pace with small pauses between sentences, never a rushed read.
- Hold each shot long enough to take in, usually 2 to 5 seconds. Don't cut faster than the viewer can follow unless it's a deliberate montage.
- Let important lines land: leave a short beat of picture after a key sentence before the next cut.
- Use natural-sounding voices. Match the voice's tone to the subject.

## 4. Music and sound
- Music sits under the voice: duck it about 10 to 15 dB while anyone is speaking.
- Fade music in and out over about a second. Never end on an abrupt audio cut unless it's the joke.
- No audio pops at cuts: add a few milliseconds of crossfade on every audio edit.
- Use only music and clips the owner has the rights to use.

## 5. Endings
- End with an ending that fits the format: a trailer gets trailer-style credits, a short gets a clean final beat, an explainer gets a short wrap-up.
- Only add labels such as "parody" or "AI-generated" when they are actually required or the owner asks.
- Hold the final frame about 1 to 2 seconds so it doesn't feel cut off.

## 6. Final check before delivering
Watch or review the whole video once and confirm:
- [ ] No black frames or dips to black between shots
- [ ] Every piece of text is on screen at least 2 seconds and readable
- [ ] Narration is unhurried and in sync with the picture
- [ ] Music is under the voice, with no pops or abrupt endings
- [ ] Nothing important is cut off at the edges
- [ ] The ending fits the format

If any box fails, fix it before sending. Tell the owner briefly what you checked.
